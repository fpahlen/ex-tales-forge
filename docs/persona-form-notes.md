## Per-persona synthesis

### Hawk — strong agreement; not thin

- **Agree:** needs real challenge and felt risk; progression after hard wins; quits if too easy, invincible, or nothing happens.
- **Disagree / nuance:** one voice also quits if *too hard* (death too often) — hard-but-fair band, not meat grinder.
- **Still thin:** fewer invented delight scenes than Paul; one respondent left delight blank.

### Paul — strong agreement; richest examples

- **Agree:** story and in-character play first; quits when dice/mechanics erase good roleplay or actions do not matter.
- **Disagree / nuance:** dice still needed for believable skills, but must not steal story time.
- **Still thin:** little on death beyond earlier interview ("good death").

### Lotta — partial agreement; thinnest overall

- **Agree (where answered):** character and world consistency; NPC reactions to *who she is*; progress of personhood, not only skills; escape / adventure-time feeling.
- **Disagree / nuance:** one founder rated description match **1** (does not know this player type well) and left delight/description-change blank. Explicit description tweak: **"different person"**, not necessarily "better".
- **Still thin:** voice/examples sparse; death/revival feelings still mostly from interview + cross-cutting ratings; Fredrik's Lotta answers are thin.

### Lars — solid agreement on action/quest; world-impact still interview-led

- **Agree:** action must happen; clear goals/quests that can complete; visible personal progress; quits on grind, inaction, or aimless sandbox.
- **Disagree / nuance:** interview stressed world impact; form answers leaned harder on finishable quests and progress indicators. One view floated GM tipping odds so progress stays possible (tension with Hawk).
- **Still thin:** delight examples uneven; one blank delight; "impact on the world" less filled out in the form than in the interview.

### Ronny — useful exploit list; match scores mixed

- **Agree:** will try AI sweet-talk, economy/"win by wealth", rules accounting, cheats/optimal builds.
- **Disagree / nuance:** response style — immutable world + no PC-unknown info vs in-story challenge vs rule-violation notifications (combine, don't pick one).
- **Still thin:** match scores 4 / 5 / 3; less shared lived example of this player than of Hawk/Paul.

## Recommendation for the next form

Do **not** ask respondents to invent open-ended gameplay moments from scratch.

Instead:

1. **Give short example play scenes** (2–4 sentences each), drawn from [examples-of-play.md](examples-of-play.md) or new drafts.
2. For each persona × scene, ask a **scale rating** of how that persona would feel, e.g. **frustrated → delighted** (and optionally a one-line why).
3. Keep a few closed questions on death/revival and dice defaults to confirm or update the leans above.
4. Optionally tag which persona(s) the respondent plays as (already useful).

That keeps the good structure of this form while removing the hardest cognitive load and filling the thin delight/voice cells with comparable data.
