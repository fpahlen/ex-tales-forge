# When Play Begins...

*A spring morning in the fifth year of King Clovis IV's reign*

---

## The Political Landscape

The Kingdom of Merovingia stirs with the unease of a realm held together by mutual suspicion rather than loyalty. King Clovis IV, five years into a reign he never earned and cannot control, obsesses over a prophecy that the Aurelian wizards have conveniently "discovered" — one that declares him destined for greatness rivaling Clovis the First.

Behind every throne stands a shadow. **Lord Chancellor Malaric** — who has served three kings, some whisper impossibly — guides the young king's hand to sign whatever needs signing. The **Queen Mother Isabeau** schemes to marry her son advantageously, with three candidates currently being evaluated. **Sir Percival the Bright**, Captain of the Royal Guard, notices strange visitors to Malaric's chambers but loves his king too much to speak his doubts.

---

## The Twelve Baronies

### The Coastal Powers

**Baroness Elara Thorne** of Aquilon watches from Port-Vent's fog-shrouded towers. Her naval blockade against Marécage has been effective — too effective. Baroness Sable Moreau's people starve, and Moreau is not one to forget. Thorne knows poison is coming; she simply doesn't know when or how.

**Baron Rolf Girard** of Ventd'or tends his swift horses on the western steppes, troubled by river-damming disputes with Ferroigne that threaten his grazing lands. He prefers diplomacy but knows his cavalry could end the argument swiftly if needed.

### The Industrial Heart

**Baron Thrain Leclerc** of Ferroigne barely contains his excitement. A major ore vein has been discovered near the Sylvaine border — a fortune in iron, if he can extract it before the forest baroness objects. His dwarven advisors counsel patience, warning of ancient wards, but patience is not Thrain's strength.

**Baroness Mira Laurent** of Rochedur sits in her stone halls, contemplating leverage. Her masons have unearthed Aurelian documents that could prove the Beaumont bloodline "impure" — elven-tainted, unfit to inherit. She hasn't decided whether to use this weapon yet, or against whom.

### The Forest Realms

**Baroness Lirael Duval** of Sylvaine prepares illusions. Ferroigne's expansion threatens her border wards, and her elven advisors are divided on response. Some counsel direct action; others prefer subtle misdirection. Lirael, smoke-like as always, prepares for both.

**Baron Eldric Fontaine** of Arborin communes with the tree ents, his patience wearing thin. Rochedur's quarrying has crept toward sacred groves — an offense he considers unforgivable. The ents counsel slow action, but Eldric's human heart burns hotter.

### The Beaumont Cousins

In **Montfort**, **Baron Garrick Beaumont** plays the jovial lord while worry gnaws at him. His grandmother Marguerite pressures him to marry; Rochedur holds documents that could expose his elven heritage; and somewhere in the shadows, his cousin Nyra hunts a portrait that could destroy them all.

In **Vallombre**, **Baroness Nyra Beaumont** has dispatched her best agents to Lumière. Baron Alain has somehow acquired a portrait of a young Marguerite — ears undeniably pointed. If the connection is made, the carefully hidden truth of Beaumont blood becomes a weapon anyone can wield. The portrait must be destroyed, and perhaps the investigator with it.

### The Extreme Realms

**Baroness Isolde Blanc** of Glacière watches the spring thaw with cold calculation. Key ice-bridges are weakening; frost trolls demand compensation for the warming. Resources are limited. Survival is the only concern in the frozen north.

**Baron Drakon Roux** of Pyremont attends to volcanic rumblings beneath Summit Keep. Fire elementals stir in the deep, restless for reasons even his salamander advisors cannot explain. He has little interest in southern politics while his mountain threatens to wake.

**Baroness Sable Moreau** of Marécage smiles with nightshade-stained lips. Aquilon's blockade has pushed her people to starvation, and she has perfected seventeen new poisons since winter. The marsh is patient. Revenge, when it comes, will be silent and absolute.

**Baron Alain de Lumière** investigates rumors of shadow magic at his court — tendrils of Vallombre's influence, he suspects. His chivalrous nature prevents the covert action such investigation requires. He is troubled by enemies who fight without honor.

---

## The Great Guilds

At Couronne, three ambassadors circle the throne like patient vultures.

**Master Dolgrim Ironvow** of the Miners Guild operates from their Ferroigne embassy, negotiating expansion into Glacière's rare mineral deposits. Blunt and transactional, he finds the court's games tedious but necessary.

**Lady Serina Vex** of the Grey Consortium maintains her pleasant smile from the Montfort embassy while waging cold war against Nyra Beaumont's shadow network. The Triumvirate Compact prevents open conflict; everything else is fair game.

**Magister Corvinus Ashborne** of the Ancient Aurelias has the king's ear — a dangerous position. His "discovered" prophecy declares Clovis destined for greatness, and the king believes every word. What Corvinus actually wants remains wrapped in scholarly rhetoric.

## The Rumors

Whispers carry on spring winds:

*"Lord Chancellor Malaric has served three kings. Three! How can any man live so long?"*

*"The Beaumont cousins have elven blood, you know. That's why they're so... peculiar."*

*"The King has received a prophecy declaring him the greatest ruler since Clovis the First!"*

*"Before the Triumvirate, there was a fourth guild — the Navigators. Some say they still exist, watching from the waves."*

*"The Queen Mother has three brides in mind for Clovis. War might come from whoever is rejected."*

*"Don't eat anything from the marshes. Baroness Moreau poisons everything she exports."*

---

## Tensions at Breaking Point

Six conflicts simmer toward violence:

1. **Aquilon vs. Marécage** — Blockade and starvation breed assassination plots
2. **Arborin vs. Rochedur** — Sacred groves threatened by quarrying
3. **Ferroigne vs. Sylvaine** — Mining expansion disturbs ancient wards
4. **Lumière vs. Vallombre** — Personal hatred after exposed lineage
5. **Montfort vs. Rochedur** — Documents that could end a dynasty
6. **Vallombre vs. Grey Consortium** — Cold war for control of the shadows

Any of these could ignite. All of them together could burn the kingdom.

---

## And So It Begins

The spring sun rises over Merovingia. In Couronne, a young king dreams of prophecy. In twelve baronies, twelve rulers plot and scheme. In embassy halls, three guilds advance agendas that dwarf any single kingdom.

Somewhere, a player character is about to step into this world.

What they do next will change everything.
