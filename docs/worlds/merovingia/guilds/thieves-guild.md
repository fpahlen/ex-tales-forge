## Overview

The Grey Consortium maintains an elaborate fiction: they are a legitimate merchant cooperative specializing in "difficult logistics." Everyone knows the truth. No one can prove it.

From their headquarters in the port city of Velantis, the Consortium's influence extends across every kingdom that has anything worth stealing—which is to say, all of them. They deal in information as readily as goods, and their networks make the Beaumonts' spy operation look provincial.

Unlike the other guilds, the Consortium values diversity absolutely. Humans, halflings, elves, dwarves, orcs—if you can steal, lie, or kill with sufficient skill, they'll take you. Talent is the only membership requirement.

- **Artifact Acquisition** - Aurelian ruins contain things people will pay dearly for
- **Blackmail Archives** - Files on everyone who matters

### The Vallombre Cold War

For decades, the Grey Consortium operated freely in Merovingia. Then Baroness Nyra Beaumont consolidated her mother's network into something that threatened their monopoly.

Now two shadow empires wage quiet war:

**What the Consortium wants:**
- Vallombre's informant networks absorbed or destroyed
- Nyra discredited or eliminated
- Restoration of their information monopoly

**What Nyra wants:**
- The Consortium expelled from Merovingia
- Their archives on her family destroyed
- Recognition as the realm's sole shadow power

**Current state:**
- Neither can destroy the other without exposing themselves
- Occasional assassinations disguised as accidents
- Recruitment wars for skilled agents
- Reluctant cooperation when mutual benefit demands it
- Elaborate rules of engagement neither will acknowledge exist

## Embassy in Montfort

The "Grey Merchant House" in Montfort's trade district maintains perfect cover. Legitimate trade operations generate real profit; the embassy pays taxes, employs locals, and files proper manifests. Baron Garrick Beaumont surely suspects the truth—but the Consortium has been careful never to move against him directly. The irony of housing their embassy in his smuggling partner's territory is not lost on anyone.

**Embassy Features:**
- Warehouses with hidden compartments

- Coded messaging system using legitimate trade correspondence
- Panic routes to extract agents if compromised

### At Court

Serina plays the flattering merchant, showering Clovis with exotic gifts and strategic compliments. She knows his secrets—all of them—and he suspects this, which terrifies him. He cannot refuse her audience because she might share what she knows. This gives her leverage no amount of gold could buy.

She never threatens directly. The threat is in what remains unsaid.

## Plot Hooks

### The Recruitment Offer

A well-dressed stranger approaches a party member in a tavern—claims to represent "interested parties" who value discretion. The favor is small: deliver a package across town. No questions asked. Payment is generous. A week later, another stranger appears with another small job. The payments increase. The favors become more complex. By the time the party realizes they're working for the Grey Consortium, they're already entangled—and they've never met Lady Serina. She only reveals herself once they've proven trustworthy through a dozen anonymous jobs... or when they have no choice but to continue.

### The Marguerite File

A nervous broker approaches the party in a market square, speaking of "valuable documents" concerning a certain prominent lady's true nature—her elven connections, her influence over both Beaumont baronies. The broker claims to represent collectors of sensitive information. The price is steep, but the implications are staggering. Only if the party investigates the broker's background will they discover the trail leads to a Montfort merchant house... and only much later might they learn whose hand truly guides these revelations.

### The Turf War Escalation

A Consortium agent is killed in Whisperdale. A Vallombre spy dies in Montfort. The Cold War threatens to turn hot. An anonymous courier reaches the party with a warning: someone is deliberately escalating, and both sides want to know who. The note offers payment for information—but who sent it? The Grey Consortium? The Beaumonts? Or the third party who benefits from open conflict?

### The Double Agent

A nervous courier finds the party with an urgent message: someone needs extraction from Whisperdale before dawn. The note is unsigned but promises substantial payment and mentions a safe house location. Only if the party succeeds do they discover they've just extracted a highly placed Vallombre agent who is actually Consortium—and now unknown powers have leverage over them. The party has done a favor for someone, but they may never learn for whom... unless they prove useful enough to warrant an introduction.
