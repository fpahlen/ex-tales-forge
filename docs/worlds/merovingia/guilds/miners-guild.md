## Overview

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
The oldest of the three great guilds, the Brethren claim origins predating human civilization—and given dwarven longevity, this may be literally true. They control mining operations, ore pricing, and tunnel engineering across a dozen kingdoms. Where others see rock, they see wealth waiting to be extracted.

The Guild is dwarf-dominated but pragmatically inclusive. Human engineers, halfling surveyors, and even the occasional orc excavator work alongside dwarven masters. Skill and loyalty matter more than blood—though the highest ranks remain firmly dwarven.

## Organization

### The Iron Council

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
The Guild's ruling body meets in Irondeep's deepest halls. Seven Master Delvers make decisions by consensus, each representing a major mining region. Merovingia falls under the Eastern Reach—Master Dolgrim's jurisdiction.

## Interests in Merovingia

### Primary Assets

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
- **Ferroigne's Iron** - The kingdom's richest deposits, extracted through partnership with Baron Thrain
- **Rochedur's Stone** - High-quality marble and granite for construction across the continent
- **Glacière's Rarities** - Gems and minerals found only in frozen earth

### The Aurelian Question

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Many Aurelian ruins extend deep underground—and the Guild claims all subterranean resources by ancient right. This puts them in direct conflict with the Ancient Aurelias, who insist these sites are magical heritage, not mining prospects.

Current disputes include:
- The Sunken Coliseum beneath Ferroigne (ore-rich lower levels)
- The Frozen Delve in Glacière (crystal formations of unclear purpose)
- The Flooded Mines of Marécage (nobody wants these, but principle demands they fight for them)

## Embassy in Ferroigne

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
The Guild maintains a formal embassy in Forgehold, integrated with Baron Thrain's mining operations. Unlike other baronies where guilds are tolerated outsiders, in Ferroigne the Miners are practically partners.

**Embassy Features:**
- Engineering workshops rivaling the Baron's own forges
- A tunnel network connecting to official guild routes (and some unofficial ones)
- Warehouses for ore transit and equipment storage
- Guest quarters for visiting guild members

## Ambassador: Master Dolgrim Ironvow

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
**Race**: Dwarf  
**Age**: 187 (middle-aged by dwarven reckoning)  
**Role**: Master Delver of the Eastern Reach

### Appearance

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Dolgrim is built like a boulder—short, impossibly broad, with arms thick as tree trunks. His beard is iron-grey, braided with small metal tokens representing mines he's opened. His eyes are permanently squinted from decades underground, and he walks with the rolling gait of someone more comfortable in tunnels than open air.

### At Court

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Royal observers -->
Dolgrim attends royal functions with barely concealed boredom. He offers King Clovis gold in exchange for mining rights, speaks plainly, and makes no effort to flatter. Clovis finds him "tedious but useful"—the dwarf's gold is good, and his bluntness is almost refreshing compared to scheming courtiers.

Dolgrim knows exactly how little Clovis matters and treats him accordingly.

### Views on Other Ambassadors

## Guild Secrets

Things the Miners don't advertise:

### The Deep Roads

<!-- TRUTH: CONTESTED | resolution: discoverable -->
<!-- HIDDEN: Players can discover these tunnels through exploration, befriending guild members, or finding old maps -->
A network of tunnels connecting mining sites across multiple kingdoms—including routes beneath Merovingia that even the barons don't know about. Officially for ore transport. Unofficially, useful for moving people and goods without surface interference.

### The Collapsed Holds

<!-- TRUTH: CONTESTED | resolution: eternal -->
<!-- Note: What was found in these holds is intentionally unknowable - the mystery IS the point -->
Some dwarven settlements delved too deep and found things best left buried. The Guild maintains these sites as "depleted mines"—sealed, guarded, and never discussed.

### The Iron Bank

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
The Guild's wealth rivals nations. They lend to kings, fund wars, and call in debts when convenient.

<!-- TRUTH: CONTESTED | resolution: discoverable -->
Several Merovingian barons owe them money.

## Plot Hooks

<!-- Note: Plot hooks are GM tools, not canonical truths -->

### The Stolen Shipment

Three ore caravans have been hijacked in Montfort. Dolgrim suspects the Grey Consortium and wants evidence—or "more direct" resolution. Baron Garrick claims innocence, but his territory, his problem.

### The Deep Claim

Miners have broken into an Aurelian chamber beneath Ferroigne containing both valuable ore AND what appears to be an intact magical laboratory. The Ancient Aurelias demand access. Dolgrim refuses. Baron Thrain is caught between profitable allies.

### The Old Debt

Baroness Isolde of Glacière's grandfather borrowed heavily from the Guild. Dolgrim has the contracts. He's not calling the debt—yet. But he might trade forgiveness for certain... considerations.

### The Lost Delve

<!-- TRUTH: CONTESTED | resolution: eternal -->
<!-- Note: What was found is intentionally unknowable - the mystery creates endless adventure possibilities -->
Guild records mention a "Master Delve" abandoned centuries ago somewhere in Merovingia—a mine so rich it could shift continental power. The records also mention why it was abandoned: something was found. Dolgrim wants to know what.
