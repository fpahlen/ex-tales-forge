# House Beaumont

*"Roots in the earth, eyes in the shadow"*

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
The Beaumonts are unique among Merovingia's ruling families—two cousins who control separate baronies yet maintain genuine trust in a realm where blood ties mean little. Their bond, anchored by a living matriarch with secrets of her own, makes them the most stable alliance in the kingdom. It also makes them the most tempting target.

## Lady Marguerite Beaumont - The Grandmother

![Lady Marguerite Beaumont](./images/marguerite-beaumont.png)

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
**Race:** Half-elf (carefully hidden)
**Age:** 78 (appears 55 due to elven blood)
**Residence:** Winters at Misthollow Keep in Vallombre; summers at Thornhaven Manor in Montfort

### The Secret

<!-- TRUTH: CONTESTED | resolution: discoverable -->
<!-- HIDDEN: The full bloodline can be discovered through investigation, ancient documents, or confronting family members -->
Lady Marguerite is the daughter of Lord Edric Beaumont and an elven woman named Aelindra from the forests that now border Sylvaine. This union, kept secret for decades, means:

- **Garrick** is quarter-elven (barely noticeable—slightly pointed ears hidden by his hair, unusually keen eyesight)
- **Nyra** is quarter-elven plus maintains contact with her grandmother's elven relatives (the "half-elf kin" referenced in Vallombre's records)
- The "shadowed lineage" Baron Alain de Lumière exposed during his botched assassination attempt refers to this elven blood

<!-- TRUTH: CONTESTED | resolution: discoverable -->
Marguerite's elven mother, Aelindra, still lives deep in Sylvaine's forests—a fact known only to Marguerite, Nyra, and the Baroness Lirael Duval (who uses this knowledge as quiet leverage).

### Personality

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Family and close servants -->
- **Sharp as a stiletto** despite her apparent frailty
- **Plays the doddering grandmother** when convenient, then delivers cutting observations
- **Remembers the old ways** when elves walked freely in Merovingia
- **Fiercely protective** of both grandchildren, though she worries more about Nyra's isolation
- **Keeper of secrets** accumulated over six decades of observation

### Role in the Family

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Marguerite serves as:
- **Arbiter** between the cousins when disagreements arise
- **Message relay** through her network of old servants
- **Living archive** of family secrets, blackmail material, and political debts
- **The one person** both Garrick and Nyra trust completely

Her death would remove the only neutral ground between two very different personalities.

### Likes

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Those who know her -->
- Watching her grandchildren succeed through cooperation
- Tending her herb garden (knowledge passed from her elven mother)
- Visitors who underestimate her
- Letters from her mother, delivered by Sylvaine's foresters

### Dislikes

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Those who know her -->
- Baron Alain de Lumière (for exposing her granddaughter)
- Those who threaten her family's unity
- Being confined to bed when politics demand attention
- The slow erosion of human-elven relations

### Garrick's Father: Lord Aldric Beaumont

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Aldric was the elder child—jovial, politically savvy, and devoted to Montfort's agricultural legacy. He married a merchant's daughter from Ventd'or, inheriting the barony at 35.

<!-- TRUTH: CONTESTED | resolution: eternal -->
<!-- Note: Whether Aldric was poisoned is intentionally unknowable - the mystery creates tension -->
His death from a wasting illness (some whisper poison from Rochedur) elevated Garrick at age 15.

### Nyra's Mother: Lady Celeste Beaumont

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Celeste married into a minor Vallombre house and vanished into its shadow politics. She excelled at espionage, building the network Nyra would inherit. Her death—officially a riding accident, actually an assassination by rivals—hardened young Nyra into the phantom she is today.

## Complementary Strengths

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
The Beaumont cousins operate as two halves of a whole:

| Garrick Provides | Nyra Provides |
|------------------|---------------|
| Food security for Vallombre's sparse population | Intelligence on rivals and emerging threats |
| Legitimate trade routes and merchant contacts | Elimination of problematic competitors |
| Public diplomatic cover and social legitimacy | Hidden leverage and blackmail material |
| Gold from agricultural surplus | Protection of family secrets |
| A cheerful public face for joint ventures | The knife in the dark when charm fails |

### The Smuggling Partnership

<!-- TRUTH: CONTESTED | resolution: discoverable -->
Their official alliance—"grain-for-iron exchanges"—masks a sophisticated smuggling operation:
- Montfort's wagons carry Vallombre's illicit goods beneath grain shipments
- Vallombre's spies protect Montfort's caravans from bandits and rivals
- Profits are split evenly, managed through grandmother's trusted servants
- Neither has ever cheated the other (unprecedented in Merovingia)

## The Bond

### Childhood

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Family memory -->
Garrick and Nyra spent summers together at Thornhaven Manor under Marguerite's watchful eye. These years forged their trust:
- **Garrick** taught Nyra to laugh, to enjoy simple pleasures
- **Nyra** taught Garrick to observe, to see the knife before it falls
- **Marguerite** taught both the value of family above politics

### Adult Relationship

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
- Garrick is one of **three people** who have seen Nyra's true face as an adult (the others are Marguerite and Nyra's personal servant)
- They communicate through **coded messages** delivered by grandmother's old servants
- They meet in person **twice yearly** at Thornhaven Manor, always in secret
- Neither has **ever broken faith** with the other—unique among Merovingia's rulers

### Tensions Between Them

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Internal family knowledge -->
Despite their bond, they differ:
- **Garrick** wishes Nyra would find happiness, perhaps marry
- **Nyra** thinks Garrick is too trusting, too visible
- **Garrick** wants to legitimize their operations over time
- **Nyra** believes legitimacy is a trap for fools
- Both worry the other will die without an heir

## The Secret Vulnerability

### Primary: Lady Marguerite

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Rival barons who investigate -->
Other barons know the Beaumonts are unusually close but don't understand why. Those who discover Marguerite's existence and importance could:

- **Kidnap her** to control both baronies simultaneously
- **Assassinate her** to shatter the cousins' unity
- **Forge communications** in her name to sow distrust
- **Threaten her** to force political concessions

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Marguerite's age makes this threat increasingly urgent. Nyra has tripled her grandmother's guard; Garrick has prepared hidden refuges across Montfort.

### Secondary: The Elven Lineage

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Those who might exploit this -->
If the full truth of the Beaumont bloodline were exposed:

- **Religious factions** might question Beaumont legitimacy to rule
- **Rival barons** could unite against "tainted blood"
- **Elven enclaves** might demand recognition or political favors
- **Nyra's human persona** would collapse entirely
- **Sylvaine's Baroness Lirael** could be implicated in the cover-up

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Baron Alain de Lumière exposed only a fragment—Nyra's connection to half-elven contacts. He doesn't yet know about Marguerite or the direct bloodline.

### Tertiary: Their Predictability

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Strategic observers -->
Because they never betray each other, rivals can anticipate:

- Montfort will **always** support Vallombre (and vice versa)
- Neither will make a move that **endangers** the other
- Threatening one **effectively threatens** both
- Their alliance is **unbreakable** but also **inflexible**

Smart enemies use this predictability to herd them into traps.

## Wedge-Driving Schemes

### Baron Alain de Lumière (Lumière)

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Lumière's inner circle -->
Already exposed Nyra's "shadowed lineage" during a botched assassination attempt. Now he seeks:
- Proof of Marguerite's existence to understand the full picture
- Evidence of the elven bloodline to destroy both cousins
- Any crack in their unity he can exploit

**Current scheme:** Placing agents in Montfort's servant class, hoping someone will mention the grandmother.

### Baroness Mira Laurent (Rochedur)

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Rochedur's archivists -->
Her masons have discovered old Aurelian records in underground vaults. These documents mention:
- "House Beaumont" receiving land grants from the Aurelian Empire
- Conditions of inheritance tied to "pure human lineage"
- Potentially invalidating claims if the bloodline is "impure"

**Current scheme:** Authenticating the documents before revealing them at a Council of Barons.

### Baroness Elara Thorne (Aquilon)

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Aquilon's court -->
Still bitter over broken alliance negotiations with Montfort. She would love to see Garrick humiliated but hesitates to strike at family bonds she herself values.

**Current scheme:** Watching and waiting, gathering information for the right moment.

### Forged Intelligence

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Potential schemers -->
Any sufficiently clever baron might plant false evidence:
- "Garrick" sold information to Rochedur
- "Nyra" ordered a hit on one of Garrick's halfling advisors
- "Marguerite" secretly favors one grandchild over the other
- The smuggling partnership is being skimmed by one side

The cousins' trust makes them resistant but not immune to such manipulation.

## The Succession Question

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Neither Garrick nor Nyra has produced an heir. This creates escalating tension:

- **Marguerite** pressures both to marry and secure the bloodline
- **Garrick** has avoided marriage, preferring fleeting romances
- **Nyra** considers herself unsuited for motherhood
- **Other barons** watch eagerly for the moment the family line ends
- **The question:** If Marguerite dies without seeing grandchildren, who inherits her accumulated secrets and alliances?

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Family discussions -->
Possible resolutions:
1. Garrick marries and produces an heir (most likely)
2. Nyra secretly has a child raised in shadow (possible but risky)
3. The cousins adopt a shared heir from a trusted family (unprecedented)
4. The family ends with them, and their alliance dies too (tragic)

## Thornhaven Manor

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
The ancestral Beaumont seat, located in Montfort's eastern hills near the Vallombre border.

### Description

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
A fortified manor house of grey stone, half-covered in ivy, with:
- **Hidden passages** connecting all major rooms
- **A warded cellar** containing family archives
- **Herb gardens** tended by Marguerite herself
- **A watchtower** with views into both baronies
- **Secret exits** known only to family members

### Current Status

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
- Maintained by a skeleton staff of trusted elderly servants
- Officially "abandoned" and "too expensive to restore"
- Actually the most secure location in either barony
- Where Garrick and Nyra meet twice yearly
- Where Marguerite spends her summers

## Plot Hooks

<!-- Note: Plot hooks are GM tools, not canonical truths -->

### The Grandmother's Illness

Marguerite has fallen gravely ill. Both cousins race to her bedside, abandoning their duties. This presents opportunities:
- Rivals could strike at undefended territories
- Someone could intercept the cousins en route
- A healer might demand dangerous payment
- The illness might be poison, requiring investigation

### The Forged Letter

A letter surfaces, apparently from Nyra to a Rochedur agent, discussing betraying Garrick. It's a forgery—but who made it, and can the cousins prove it before trust fractures?

### The Elven Claim

Aelindra, Marguerite's mother, sends word from Sylvaine: she is dying and wishes to see her daughter and great-grandchildren before the end. But such a journey would expose the family secret...

### The Succession Ultimatum

Marguerite gives both grandchildren one year to produce an heir or she will choose one for them. The candidates she's considering come from families with their own agendas.

### The Lumière Discovery

Baron Alain's agents have found a portrait of young Marguerite—with unmistakably pointed ears. He doesn't yet know who she is, but he's searching for answers.
