# Barony of Marécage

*Eastern marshlands of poison, ambush, and orc-allied swamp warfare*

## Atmosphere

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
The eastern marshes swallow sound. Mangroves twist upward like tortured hands; fog lies so thick that a man may walk for hours and never know he is lost. Submerged Aurelian roads disappear into black water where pale things move beneath the surface.

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Court observers -->
Baroness Sable Moreau smiles with lips stained the color of nightshade. Orc shamans teach her poisons that kill without sound. She finds dwarven rigidity laughable and useless in the swamp. Vallombre understands her darkness; Aquilon she resents for the ships that choke her rivers and starve her people.

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->

- **Tangled mangroves** hiding ambush points

- **Submerged Aurelian road ruins** teeming with undead
- **Orc longhouse settlements** on stilts

## Capital: Mudfort

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
A stilted village characterized by:
- Reed hut construction near orc longhouses
- Built over flooded ancient mosaics
- Maze-like walkways requiring local guides
- Constant mist and the smell of decay
