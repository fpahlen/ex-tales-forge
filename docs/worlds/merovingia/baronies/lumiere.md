# Barony of Lumière

*Sun-blessed southwestern realm of radiant knights and enchanted vineyards*

## Atmosphere

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Visitors and residents -->
Southwestern hills glow golden under a kinder sun. Vineyards march in perfect rows; crystal lakes mirror skies so blue they hurt the eyes. Pristine Aurelian amphitheaters stand open to the air, their stages sometimes visited by spectral actors who play scenes no living audience remembers.

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Court observers -->
Baron Alain de Lumière rides at the head of knights whose armor catches light like liquid silver. Elven scholars in seclusion fill his library with dangerous beauty. He cannot abide orc filth spreading across clean land. Sylvaine shares his love of light and learning; Vallombre he considers dangerous for every poisoned rumor that darkens his court.
