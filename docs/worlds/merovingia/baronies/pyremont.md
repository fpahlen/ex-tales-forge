# Barony of Pyremont

*Southern mountain fortress realm of fire magic and impregnable defenses*

## Atmosphere

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Southern mountains rise in sharp, flame-colored pyramids, their peaks crowned with perpetual firelight. Fiery chasms breathe heat; Aurelian villas cling to cliffs like stubborn ivy, their terraces carved by hands long turned to dust.

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Court observers -->
Baron Drakon Roux laughs amid the roar of forges and the scream of eagles. Dwarf engineers in the deep holds feed his hunger for war machines. Subtle games bore him—he wants steel on steel. Ferroigne is his brother-in-arms; Aquilon he resents for every storm that drowns his passes and wakes angry ghosts.
