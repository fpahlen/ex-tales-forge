# Barony of Aquilon

*Northern coastal realm of storms, prophecy, and elven-blessed naval supremacy*

## Atmosphere

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Winds howl across the northern cliffs where black waves smash against jagged rock like war drums. Fog clings to everything, thick and cold, broken only by the unearthly blue glow of ancient sea-crystals that pulse in the depths of half-drowned Aurelian harbors. Port-Vent reeks of salt, tar and fish; its lighthouses stab the mist with beams that never fade.

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Court observers -->
Baroness Elara Thorne rules from a high tower, her eyes sharp as a gull's, mind sharpened by dreams her elven husband slips into her sleep. She spins webs of words and promises, despising blunt steel and slippery halfling coin-changers. Sylvaine is her shield against the orc tide; Montfort she regards with the cold distrust of one who remembers broken oaths.
