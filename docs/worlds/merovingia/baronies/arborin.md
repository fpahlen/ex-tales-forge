# Barony of Arborin

*Southeastern wooded mountains where living forests defend the realm*

## Atmosphere

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Southeastern mountains wear cloaks of ancient forest. Waterfalls roar down cliffs into emerald pools; Aurelian villas lie half-swallowed by vines, their marble whispering with the voices of tree ents that walk when humans sleep.

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Court observers -->
Baron Eldric Fontaine stands among living giants, steady as the oldest root. His elven wife has taught him the patient fury of the wood. He cannot forgive axes and smoke. Sylvaine is his kin in spirit; Rochedur he views as a persistent threat for every quarry that has torn sacred groves bleeding.
