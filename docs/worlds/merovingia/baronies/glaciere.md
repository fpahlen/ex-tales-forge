# Barony of Glacière

*Northeastern frozen realm of ice magic and frost troll alliances*

## Atmosphere

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
The northeast is a land of eternal winter. Glacial fjords cut deep blue wounds into white; Aurelian bridges lie frozen mid-span across chasms, their stone cracked by centuries of frost.

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Court observers -->
Baroness Isolde Blanc walks the ice in furs white as bone, her mind colder than the wind. Frost-troll kin teach her spells that preserve flesh and malice alike. Warm lands disgust her. Pyremont stands with her against southern greed; Lumière she views as a threat after the sun-blast that once cracked her palace walls.
