# Barony of Ventd'or

*Western steppes of swift cavalry and wind magic*

## Atmosphere

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Western steppes stretch forever under a sky full of wind. Tall grasses bow and rise like a living sea; eroded Aurelian aqueducts stand like broken ribs, channeling breezes that never rest.

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Court observers -->
Baron Rolf Girard rides at the head of a thunder of hooves, hair whipping like a banner. Halfling nomads teach him the language of open sky. Enclosed mountains feel like cages to him. Montfort trades him grain for swift horses; Ferroigne he resents for every dam that stills the wind he loves.
