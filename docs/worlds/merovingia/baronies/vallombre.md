# Barony of Vallombre

*Shadow-shrouded valleys where spies and assassins rule from the mist*

## Atmosphere

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Thick fog lies on the river valleys like a living shroud. Gorges twist like the coils of some great serpent; orc fires flicker far off in the gloom. Sunken Aurelian baths reflect twisted faces in black water that never warms.

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Court observers -->
Baroness Nyra Beaumont is a shadow among shadows, her smile as thin and sharp as a stiletto. Half-elf cousins speak to her from places no human foot may tread. She despises battles fought in daylight. Montfort's roads carry her secrets; Lumière she considers a persistent threat after the botched knife that exposed her shadowed lineage.

### Family

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Nyra is one half of the Beaumont cousins—the only genuinely trusting alliance among Merovingia's rulers. Her cousin [Baron Garrick Beaumont](./montfort.md) controls Montfort, and together they operate under the guidance of their grandmother, [Lady Marguerite Beaumont](../families/beaumont.md).

<!-- TRUTH: CONTESTED | resolution: discoverable -->
She inherited Vallombre at age 17 after her mother Lady Celeste's assassination by political rivals. Her quarter-elven blood—inherited through her grandmother—is the source of the "shadowed lineage" Baron Alain de Lumière exposed. Her "half-elf kin" are actually relatives from her grandmother Marguerite's elven mother, who still lives deep in Sylvaine's forests.
<!-- HIDDEN: The identity of Lady Celeste's assassins remains undiscovered -->

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Garrick is one of only three people who have seen Nyra's true face as an adult (the others being Marguerite and her personal servant).

## Notable Locations

### Misthollow Keep

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
A fortress hidden in Vallombre's deepest mists, where grandmother Marguerite spends her winters. Triply guarded by Nyra's best agents, it's the most secure location in the barony.

### Thornhaven Manor

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
The ancestral Beaumont seat in Montfort's eastern hills. Nyra travels there twice yearly to meet her cousin Garrick in secret.

## Plot Hooks

<!-- Note: Plot hooks are GM tools, not canonical truths -->

### The Succession Question

Grandmother Marguerite pressures Nyra to produce an heir, but Nyra considers herself unsuited for motherhood. She might secretly arrange for a child to be raised in shadow—or she might convince Garrick to produce an heir for both of them.

### The Portrait Discovery

Baron Alain de Lumière's agents have found a portrait of young Marguerite with unmistakably pointed ears. He doesn't yet know who she is. Nyra must destroy the portrait or the investigator before the connection is made.

### The Elven Claim

Great-grandmother Aelindra is dying in Sylvaine and wishes to see her descendants. Nyra must decide whether to risk the journey—and expose the family secret—for a final farewell.
