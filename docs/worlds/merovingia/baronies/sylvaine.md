# Barony of Sylvaine

*Northwestern woodland barony of elite archers and ancient druidic magic*

## Atmosphere

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Deep in the northwest the forest breathes. Towering oaks older than empires stretch toward a sky they almost touch, leaves whispering secrets in a language only the wind fully understands. Sun shafts pierce the canopy like golden spears; beneath them Aurelian aqueducts lie half-buried, their stone channels still carrying water that glows faintly with forgotten magic.

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Court observers -->
Baroness Lirael Duval moves among the trees like smoke, her illusions so perfect that even the birds are sometimes fooled. Ancient elven voices murmur in her ear from the deep glades. She loathes the bite of dwarven axes into living wood. Aquilon stands beside her against the outer dark; Ferroigne she blames for every spirit their delving has awakened.
