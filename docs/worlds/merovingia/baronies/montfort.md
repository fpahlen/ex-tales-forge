# Barony of Montfort

*Central agricultural heartland blessed with fertile plains and halfling expertise*

## Atmosphere

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Golden waves of wheat roll across the heartland under an enormous sky. Halfling burrows peek from gentle hillsides like curious rabbits, while ivy-choked Aurelian forums dream beneath the soil, their marble vaults holding earth-gems that hum with quiet power when touched.

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Court observers -->
Baron Garrick Beaumont laughs easily, cheeks flushed from wine and deals done in back rooms. His halfling advisors slip him whispers from hidden doorways. He chafes at elven gates slammed shut on good coin. Vallombre keeps his barns full and his coffers heavy; Rochedur he distrusts, never forgetting the sour taste of poisoned water and broken oaths.

### Family

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Garrick is one half of the Beaumont cousins—the only genuinely trusting alliance among Merovingia's rulers. His cousin [Baroness Nyra Beaumont](./vallombre.md) controls Vallombre, and together they operate under the guidance of their grandmother, [Lady Marguerite Beaumont](../families/beaumont.md).

<!-- TRUTH: CONTESTED | resolution: discoverable -->
He inherited Montfort at age 15 after his father Lord Aldric's death from a wasting illness (some whisper poison from Rochedur). His quarter-elven blood—inherited through his grandmother—manifests only as slightly pointed ears (hidden by his hair) and unusually keen eyesight.

## Notable Locations

### Thornhaven Manor

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
The ancestral Beaumont seat in the eastern hills, near the Vallombre border. Officially "abandoned," it's actually where Garrick meets his cousin Nyra twice yearly and where their grandmother Marguerite spends her summers.

## Plot Hooks

<!-- Note: Plot hooks are GM tools, not canonical truths -->

### The Succession Question

Grandmother Marguerite is pressuring Garrick to marry and produce an heir. Several families are angling to provide candidates—each with their own political agenda. Garrick has avoided commitment, preferring fleeting romances, but time is running out.

### The Rochedur Documents

Baroness Mira Laurent's masons have discovered Aurelian records that might invalidate Beaumont land claims if the family's "impure" bloodline is proven. Garrick needs those documents destroyed or discredited.

### The Forged Letter

A letter surfaces claiming Nyra plans to betray Garrick. It's a forgery—but proving it requires finding the forger before trust between the cousins fractures.
