# Barony of Rochedur

*Central highland fortress carved from living stone and ancient rune magic*

## Atmosphere

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
The central highlands are a world of stone and silence. Enormous boulders lie scattered like the toys of giants; toppled Aurelian arches guard hidden chambers where torchlight wakes carvings that have not been seen in centuries.

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Court observers -->
Baroness Mira Laurent moves through echoing halls with the grace of a blade being drawn. Her dwarf husband's knowledge of rock and rune is her greatest weapon. She finds elven whimsy tiresome and dangerous. Pyremont guards her borders; Vallombre she regards with cold calculation, never forgetting the stolen blueprints from sacred stone.
