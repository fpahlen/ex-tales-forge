# Barony of Ferroigne

*Eastern industrial powerhouse of iron, forge, and dwarven engineering*

## Atmosphere

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
The eastern badlands smell of hot iron and sulfur. Cracked red earth smokes from volcanic vents; dwarf tunnels plunge deep beneath collapsed Aurelian coliseums whose stone seats still echo with the phantom roar of long-dead crowds.

<!-- TRUTH: PERSPECTIVE | holder: Court observers -->
Baron Thrain Leclerc strides through smoke and sparks, face blackened, heart beating to the anvil's rhythm. His dwarf wife teaches him secrets older than the kingdom. Fragile elven oaths make him sneer. Pyremont shares his mountain walls; Sylvaine he considers unreliable for every choked tunnel and strangled delve.

## Strengths

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
- Heavily armored legions trained by dwarf advisors
- Impervious to most siege attacks
- Superior metalworking and engineering
- Self-sufficient in military equipment

## Weaknesses

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
- Slow mobilization due to heavy equipment
- Dependency on imported food
- Volcanic terrain can be unstable

## Geography

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
Eastern hilly badlands featuring:
- **Deep mines** extending into dwarven territory
- **Volcanic vents** providing natural forges
- **Collapsed Aurelian coliseum ruins** echoing with spectral gladiators
- **Barren hills** rich in mineral deposits

## Capital: Forgehold

<!-- TRUTH: ABSOLUTE -->
A surface citadel characterized by:
- Position above subterranean dwarf holds
- Illumination by lava flows and rune lamps from ruins
- Constant hammering from forges day and night
- Thick walls of reinforced iron and stone
