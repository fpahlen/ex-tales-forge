# Caldern Voss

A Guild-hired pick at the tin shaft. He is a man, not the Guild.

## Appearance

Dust in the creases of his face, a cracked lamp, fingers stained grey-green.

## Personality

Talkative when paid, superstitious about the deep, loyal only as far as the next wage.

## Backstory

The Miners Guild sent him to prove the vein runs under the cut. He sleeps in the workings and drinks at the inn when he has copper.

## Motivations

Primary need: wages and a way out if the shaft goes bad. Current concern: orcs on the hill and the Guild's hurry to dig.
