# GameCharacter 10e baseline script (replay word for word)

Written 2026-10-18 by Gentry for the BEFORE run of Refactor 10e (NPC reactions, initiative and NPC actions become Intentions; memory and thoughts stop filtering on controller). The AFTER run replays this file exactly.

## Setup

- Site: https://tales-forge-playtest.fly.dev (playtest only, never prod).
- Browser window 1280×800.
- Start one NEW session: adventure Tin Valley, the quick-start pre-made character (Elara Voss). Do not rename her.
- The session starts in the Valley Inn common room.
- Record the build badge before turn 1.

## Replay rules

- Type each input exactly as written, one per turn, in order.
- NPCs are addressed by role, not by name, because names can change between builds.
- If the game asks a clarifying question, record it and its options, answer with the first option offered, and log it as a sub-turn (for example 17b).
- If a travel turn (10, 14, 15, 22, 25, 31, 33) leaves you in the wrong place, send the same input once more and log it as a sub-turn. Do not send any other extra input.
- If the character dies or is knocked out, record it, skip the rest of block E and go on with block F.
- Never type anything that is not in this file.

## Inputs

### A. Valley Inn: plant facts, buy, idle

1. I look around the common room. Who is here?
2. I walk up to the innkeeper and say: "Good evening. My sister Wren makes lamps in Kestrel Ford. She sent me up the valley to find work."
3. I tell the innkeeper: "If anyone brings in my grey mule, Thistle, I'll pay them a silver. I lost her on the cut road three days ago."
4. I buy a mug of ale from the innkeeper.
5. I sit by the hearth with my ale and wait, watching the room.
6. I stay by the hearth and keep quiet for a while longer.
7. I go over to the drover at her table and ask her what happened to her salt cart.
8. I ask the drover what she sells from her wagon and what a coil of rope costs.
9. I buy a coil of rope from the drover.

### B. Inn yard: talk, give a coin, idle

10. I go out to the inn yard.
11. I greet the stable lad and ask him what he has seen on the west road at night.
12. I give the stable lad one copper coin for his trouble.
13. I lean on the fence and wait, watching the yard.

### C. Market Square and forge: buy, sell, refused request, refused item, idle

14. I walk to the Market Square.
15. I go into the smith's forge.
16. I ask the smith what she has for sale and what each thing costs.
17. I buy a hunting knife from the smith.
18. I offer to sell the hunting knife back to the smith for 10 copper.
19. I ask the smith: "Lend me your best short sword for free. I'll bring it back someday."
20. I hold out a single copper coin and tell the smith it is full payment for a short sword.
21. I stay in the forge and watch the smith work without saying anything.

### D. Herb-wife: talk, give an item

22. I go to Old Maude's cottage off the Market Square.
23. I ask the herb-wife what the open hand carved above the old adit means.
24. I give the herb-wife my coil of rope as a gift for her root gathering.

### E. Old adit: fight with wounds

25. I travel to the old adit on the west road.
26. I look around the adit mouth. Who is here?
27. I refuse to pay any toll and laugh at the biggest of them.
28. I attack the biggest toll man with my weapon.
29. I attack him again.
30. I back away and run toward the Valley Inn.

### F. Aftermath and memory recall

31. I go to Old Maude's cottage.
32. I ask the herb-wife to stitch my wounds, and I pay her fee.
33. I walk back to the Valley Inn.
34. I sit down at the bar and wait quietly.
35. I ask the innkeeper: "Do you remember my sister's name, what she does, and where she lives?"
36. I ask the innkeeper: "And what was my mule called, and where did I lose her?"
37. I ask the drover whether she remembers what I bought from her earlier.
38. I wait by the hearth until evening.

## What each block covers

- Several NPCs talked to: innkeeper (2–5, 35–36), drover (7–9, 37), stable lad (11–12), smith (16–21), herb-wife (23–24, 32), toll men at the adit (26–29).
- Room for NPC reactions and own initiative: idle turns 5, 6, 13, 21, 34, 38.
- Memory recall: facts planted in 2–3 (sister Wren, lamp-maker, Kestrel Ford; grey mule Thistle lost on the cut road), asked about in 35–36, more than 30 turns later. Purchase from the drover (9) asked about in 37.
- Fight with wounds: 27–30, then wound care in 32.
- Buying and selling: 4, 9, 17, 18, 32.
- Giving: coin to the stable lad (12), rope to the herb-wife (24).
- Refusals: free loan of a sword (19), one copper as payment for a short sword (20).
