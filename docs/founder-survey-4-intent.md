## Intro

> Hi! When a player types something vague, the game has to guess what they mean, and a wrong guess plays out wrong: a purchase nobody asked for, a fight that should have been a sneak, a trip that was only a plan.
>
> Here are 12 real and made-up player lines where we think more than one reading is fair. For each one, read the scene, then pick what **you** think the player means. This is not a test; we want your gut, as a player. If it could honestly go either way, pick **"Unclear, the game should ask the player"**: that is a useful answer, it tells us when the game should check before acting. About 10 minutes.

## How we'll use the answers

- Answers become labels in the eval fixture, as described in [`scripts/survey-intent/apply_answers.md`](scripts/survey-intent/apply_answers.md): the majority reading becomes gold, a plausible minority reading becomes acceptable, and an 'Unclear' win or no majority marks the item as one the game should ask about.
- Per-item founder agreement sets how sure Jev must be before it acts without asking (the ask-band calibration).
