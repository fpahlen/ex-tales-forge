# Founder survey 2: how we'd play (merged, not sent)

> **Merged.** This survey never went out. Its play-style questions and archetype grids are now part of the combined form in [founder survey 3](founder-survey-3.md); edit that file, not this one.

## Why this survey

We four founders are Tales Forge's first customers. This survey asks **how, when and where we'd actually play**, so we get a **first indication of usage** before anyone else plays. It's a rough guess from four people, not data. Once beta starts, **beta telemetry replaces these answers with real numbers**.

What we'll measure in beta:

- **Session length** (first to last turn in a sitting)
- **Sessions per week** per player
- **Turns per session**
- **Billed GM seconds per session** (only the time from a player's send to the GM's text coming back; idle time is free)
- **Time of day** sessions start
- **Device** (phone / tablet / laptop or desktop)
- **Time between turns** (how long players think and type between GM replies)

The answers also feed the pricing draft in [pricing draft: pool of play time](pricing-time-pool.md) (prepaid play time, only GM response time billed, about $1 per real hour; our target pattern is a 20-minute commute session).

Lesson from the first persona form: inventing gameplay examples was hard. So the play-style part only asks about **our own habits**: no made-up scenes needed. With the character-choice grids below, aim: **under 8 minutes** total to fill in.

## Form: Play style

**Form intro text:**

> Hi! This one should stay under about 8 minutes and there are no wrong answers. First we ask how *you* would fit Tales Forge into your week; then which fantasy archetypes each persona would actually want to play. Most of us play in more than one way (a bit on the bus, a long evening now and then), so pick every situation that fits you. Guesses are totally fine.

### Q1. Where and when would you play?

- **Type:** Checkboxes (pick several)
- **Required:** yes
- **Text:** Which of these situations would you play Tales Forge in? Pick all that fit.
- **Options:**
  - On the commute (bus, train)
  - Late at night at home, for hours
  - Short breaks during the day (5–10 min)
  - Long sessions on the weekend
  - With friends, as a group
  - In bed or on the couch, on my phone
  - Other (Google Forms "Other" option with free text)

### Q2. How often?

- **Type:** Multiple choice grid (one answer per row)
- **Required:** no (each row optional)
- **Text:** How often would you play in each situation? Only fill in the rows you picked above.
- **Rows:** Commute · Late night at home · Short breaks · Weekend long sessions · With friends · In bed / on the couch · Other
- **Columns:** Daily · A few times a week · Weekly · Monthly · Rarely

### Q3. How long?

- **Type:** Multiple choice grid (one answer per row)
- **Required:** no
- **Text:** How long would a typical session be in each situation? Only the rows you picked.
- **Rows:** same as Q2
- **Columns:** Under 10 min · 10–30 min · 30–60 min · 1–2 hours · More than 2 hours

### Q4. On what?

- **Type:** Checkbox grid (several answers per row allowed)
- **Required:** no
- **Text:** What would you play on in each situation? Only the rows you picked.
- **Rows:** same as Q2
- **Columns:** Phone · Tablet · Laptop / desktop

### Q5. Typing or talking?

- **Type:** Multiple choice grid (one answer per row)
- **Required:** no
- **Text:** Would you type or use your voice in each situation? Only the rows you picked.
- **Rows:** same as Q2
- **Columns:** Typing · Voice · Both

### Q6. One story everywhere?

- **Type:** Multiple choice
- **Required:** yes
- **Text:** Would you continue the same story across situations (for example, start on the bus and carry on at home tonight)?
- **Options:** Yes · No · It depends
- **Follow-up (Short answer, optional):** If it depends, on what?

### Q7. Your pace

- **Type:** Linear scale, 1–5
- **Required:** yes
- **Text:** What pace do you enjoy most?
- **Labels:** 1 = Quick, short turns · 5 = Long, rich turns

### Q8. Spending

- **Type:** Multiple choice
- **Required:** yes
- **Text:** At the amount of play you described above, how much would you expect to spend per month? (Rough guess. Prices in USD. For reference, we're thinking of about $1 per hour of game-master reply time, with waiting and thinking time free.)
- **Options:** $0 · $1–5 · $5–10 · $10–20 · $20+

### Q9. Your ideal week

- **Type:** Paragraph (long answer)
- **Required:** no
- **Text:** Describe your ideal week with Tales Forge. When, where, with whom, and for how long?
