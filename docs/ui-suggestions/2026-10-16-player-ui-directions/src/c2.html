Chapter the Second
The Widows' Purse

Rain comes off the mountain in grey sheets, and the Drover's Rest smells of wet wool and tallow. Three guildsmen hold the table nearest the fire as though they had paid for the hearth itself. By the door sits a girl of sixteen, one hand pressed flat over something under her coat. Two sellswords are still laughing at whatever she asked them.

"Half this room has watched you guard that coat." you said

Elin Harrow looks at you the way miners look at a cracked beam. "It's the widows' purse, not mine. Eleven families, and the guild says the mine owes them nothing."

When you look again, the bench by the fire holds only two men. You did not see the third one go. The door to the yard swings on its leather hinge.

"Stay close. Is there another way out?" you said

"Through the kitchen. The pot-boy showed me last night." Her hand stays on her coat. By the cards, a man in a yellow doublet smiles at nothing.
