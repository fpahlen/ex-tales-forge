The Widows' Purse

Ferrow · the Drover's Rest · day one, raining

Rain comes off the mountain in grey sheets, and the Drover's Rest smells of wet wool and tallow. Three guildsmen hold the table nearest the fire as though they had paid for the hearth itself. By the door, a girl of perhaps sixteen sits with her back to the wall, one hand pressed flat over something under her coat. Two sellswords are still laughing at whatever she asked them.

"Half this room has watched you guard that coat."

Elin Harrow looks at you the way miners look at a cracked beam. "It's the widows' purse, not mine. Eleven families, and the guild says the mine owes them nothing." Across the room, one of the guildsmen turns his head.
