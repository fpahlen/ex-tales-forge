# Time

Agreed with Fredrik, 2026-10-21.

One timeline, two units. World time moves in ticks (about a quarter hour). Tactical time, started by anything where seconds matter (combat, fleeing, a chase), moves in tocks, with 1000 tocks to a tick, and everyone in the scene advances together. When the scene ends, the tocks add up into ticks. A refused action costs nothing.
