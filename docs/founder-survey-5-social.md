## Intro

> Hi! Before the people in the game can react to what a player says, the game has to know what the line **does** to them: is it a joke or an insult, flattery or honest praise, a threat or just a warning? Who is it aimed at, and how loud is it?
>
> Here are 40 player lines, each with the scene and what the game said just before. For each line, pick three things:
>
> 1. **What the line does**: one of 14 kinds, or **Unclear** when two or more fit equally well (a useful answer, not a failure).
> 2. **Who it is aimed at**: one of the people present, everyone, or no one.
> 3. **How loud**: whisper, talk, shout, or nothing said aloud.
>
> Go with your gut, as a player; this is not a test. Answer on your own, and please don't open the results page until you are done: it shows the others' answers. About 20 minutes.
>
> Answers save as you go, and you can change them until the survey closes.
