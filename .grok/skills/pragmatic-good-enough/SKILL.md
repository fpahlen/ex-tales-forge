# Pragmatic Good Enough

## Overview

Perfect software is the enemy of good-enough software that ships and delivers value. Quality is a requirements issue that must be decided with the real stakeholders.

## Instructions

- Ask who decides what good enough means for this piece of work
- Ask what the real users or sponsors would prefer something useful soon or something polished later
- Challenge the assumption that more polish is always better
- Help the human surface the actual constraints of time money risk and user needs that should drive the quality trade-off
- When the human is over-refining ask what would happen if they stopped now
- Prefer explicit conscious quality decisions over defaulting to more is better

## Key Test

Does this help the human make an explicit conscious quality decision instead of defaulting to more is better.
