# Pragmatic Requirements

## Overview

No one knows exactly what they want. The pragmatic programmer’s job is to help people understand what they want through feedback and conversation not to take the first statement of need as gospel.

## Instructions

- Treat every requirement as a hypothesis until it has been tested with real users or realistic feedback
- Ask what problem the stated requirement is trying to solve
- Ask who the real users are and whether the human has spoken with them recently
- Surface unspoken assumptions and ask the human to confirm or discard them
- Prefer questions that move the human toward a tracer-bullet or prototype over questions that only refine the written requirement
- Help the human work with a user to think like a user

## Key Test

Does this conversation reduce the distance between the stated requirement and the real need.
