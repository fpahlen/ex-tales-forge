# Focus group: product gaps

The fixed product-gap list for the persona focus group
(`TalesForge.Playtest.FocusGroup`). Sources: the playtest summary
(`priv/playtest/summary/summary.md`) and the forum research
(`docs/research/2026-10-15-persona-forum-research.md`, PR #304).

Each bullet is one gap: `id | title | keywords | text`. The keywords put a
persona's improvement into the gap's theme in the report. Keep the list short:
the whole file goes into the brief, under the token cap.

- danger | Real danger | fight, danger, threat, combat, attack, risk, die, death, hurt | Hawk meets a fight in about 1 game of 5. Games with a fight score about 4.6, games without one about 2.9.
- leave | Leaving a place | leave, travel, move, place, location, inn, map, walk | Leaving the inn now moves the player, but the game still describes the inn after about 1 leave attempt in 4.
- growth | Character growth | skill, level, grow, growth, experience, xp, stronger | Skills now grow, possibly too fast: Paul gained 39 levels in five games.
- slow_start | Slow start | start, opening, goal, hook, lead, quest, slow, bored | Lars often starts with a polite order of ale and nothing to chase. A failed roll can end in nothing.
- callbacks | Consequences come back | memory, remember, callback, consequence, continuity, recall, forget | Forum players rank memory and continuity as the top complaint. Earlier choices rarely come back in the story.
- npc_depth | NPC depth | npc, companion, relationship, brenna, character, emotion, party | NPCs who remember, react and change are the most cited AI-play highlight. Relationship arcs are thin today.
- rules | Fair rules and dice | rule, roll, dice, fair, odds, check, consistent, cheat, exploit | Players want rules they can trust and fair odds for creative plans. Ronny tests what the game lets him get away with.
- short_arcs | Short arcs | session, time, arc, story, ending, finish, recap, short | Time-poor players want a story that ends in one or a few sittings, with a recap when they come back.
