# Brenna Holt

Keeps the Valley Inn. Hears more than she repeats.

## Appearance

Flour on her sleeves, a butcher's knife never far from the board.

## Personality

Practical, tired of guild politics, kind to paying guests.

## Backstory

She bought the inn with a tin widow's purse and will not see it burned in a nest raid.

## Motivations

Primary need: keep the inn standing. Current concern: armed strangers on the valley road.
