# Racial Mechanics Summary for Skill-Based Fantasy RPG

## Overview
This document outlines racial options for a skill-based fantasy RPG where progression is driven by learning from failures, using a 1d100 skill roll system (skills 1 to 100, success on ≤ skill level, LP-driven improvement, stats 3-18). Races (Humans, Elves, Dwarves, Gnomes, Halflings, Half-Elves) provide starting stat modifiers, skill bonuses, and traits to enhance role-playing and build diversity without overshadowing skill progression. Modifiers are modest (+1 or +2, occasional -1) to maintain balance, and skill bonuses align with the 100-150 point starting pool, capped at novice levels (≤25). Traits encourage narrative adventures (e.g., trainer quests) and integrate with failure-driven growth.

## Human
- **Description**: Versatile and resilient, humans adapt quickly, thriving on trial and error.
- **Stat Modifiers**: +1 to any two stats (player choice).
- **Skill Bonuses**: +25 starting skill points (freely distributed).
- **Traits**: **Adaptability** – Reroll one failed skill check per session (+1 LP if reroll fails).

## Elf
- **Description**: Graceful, long-lived, and attuned to nature/magic, elves learn from subtle mistakes.
- **Stat Modifiers**: +2 DEX, +1 INT or WIS (choice), -1 CON.
- **Skill Bonuses**: +15 to Ranged Combat or Arcana, +10 to Survival or Tracking.
- **Traits**: **Keen Senses** – +10 to Perception-based skills (e.g., Insight, Tracking) in natural settings; **Elven Grace** – Turn one critical failure into a normal failure once per session on DEX-linked skills (+1 LP).

## Dwarf
- **Description**: Sturdy craftsmen, dwarves endure failures in harsh conditions, forging strength.
- **Stat Modifiers**: +2 CON, +1 STR, -1 CHA.
- **Skill Bonuses**: +15 to Melee Combat or Crafting, +10 to Survival or Alchemy.
- **Traits**: **Stonecunning** – +10 to skills involving stone/earth (e.g., Climbing caves, Herbalism for minerals); **Resilience** – Reduce wounds taken by 1 (min 0) once per combat (+1 LP per wound absorbed).

## Gnome
- **Description**: Curious tinkerers, gnomes treat failures as experiments, innovating through insight.
- **Stat Modifiers**: +2 INT, +1 DEX, -1 STR.
- **Skill Bonuses**: +15 to Alchemy or Lockpicking, +10 to Arcana or Crafting.
- **Traits**: **Tinker's Insight** – +10 to INT-linked skills when experimenting or reverse-engineering; **Curious Mind** – Gain +1 LP when failing a skill check while investigating something new.

## Halfling
- **Description**: Cheerful, social, and resilient, halflings find joy in simple pleasures and navigate danger with surprising luck.
- **Stat Modifiers**: +2 DEX, +1 CHA, -1 STR.
- **Skill Bonuses**: +15 to Stealth or Cooking, +10 to Persuasion or Trading.
- **Traits**: **Lucky** – Reroll one critical failure per session (+1 LP if the reroll fails); **Brave** – +10 to resist fear effects and intimidation.

## Half-Elf
- **Description**: Bridging two worlds, half-elves combine human adaptability with elven grace, though they sometimes struggle with identity.
- **Stat Modifiers**: +1 DEX, +1 CHA.
- **Skill Bonuses**: +10 to any two skills (player choice).
- **Traits**: **Versatile Heritage** – Choose one Human or Elf trait at character creation; **Social Chameleon** – +10 to social skills when interacting with either humans or elves.

## OCEAN Personality Modifiers

These modifiers affect NPC personality generation, shaping how NPCs of each race typically behave. Traits are on a 1-10 scale; modifiers adjust the base personality.

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **O**penness | Curiosity, creativity, openness to new experiences |
| **C**onscientiousness | Organization, reliability, self-discipline |
| **E**xtraversion | Sociability, energy, talkativeness |
| **A**greeableness | Cooperation, trust, helpfulness |
| **N**euroticism | Emotional instability, anxiety, moodiness |

*These modifiers are applied to randomly generated base traits, clamped to 1-10.*
