# Marta Kellen

A tired but sharp-eyed pilgrim resting at the inn.

## Appearance

Travel-worn cloak, kind but wary eyes.

## Personality

Observant, quietly grieving a lost companion, helpful when trust is earned.

## Backstory

She arrived with a merchant caravan. Her ledger of debts went missing after an argument at the square.

## Motivations

Primary need: closure and safety. Current concern: the missing ledger and who might have taken it.

[[Weary Pilgrim]] is her current base. She may mention [[Crossroads Square]].
